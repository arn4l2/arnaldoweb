ARN4L2 (Ar-nal-do) is a producer and DJ whose work emerges from the sonic traditions of the greater Caribbean and deep listening.

Raised amidst records and picó sound system culture from his native Cartagena, his artistry blends cumbia, bullerengue, chalupa, champeta, dancehall, reggae, soca, zouk, and kompa with the futuristic energy of house music. This unique blend of genres reveals a rare and intuitive ear.

He has worked with labels such as Moonshine Records, Palenque Records, Man Recordings, and Galletas Calientes, which released his vinyl EP Caribe in 2024.

His most recent remix, a reinterpretation of Cumbia del Café Martinez by El Búho, highlights his contribution to the evolving landscape of global music.

ARN4L2’s work bridges deep-rooted Caribbean rhythms with forward-thinking production, while his impeccable DJ technique elevates the experience of his sound.
